Definitions and exclusions

Late = actual delivery date after promised date, among delivered orders with both timestamps. Cancellation denominator = all orders. Merchandise value excludes freight; neither value nor payments equal profit. Missing delivery timestamps are excluded, never treated as on time. Review score uses the latest recorded review per order. Dispatch = purchase to carrier handoff; transit = handoff to customer. Negative stage durations are excluded.
