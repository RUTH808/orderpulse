# OrderPulse | Delivery & Customer Experience Analytics

Python, SQL, DuckDB, pandas, Dimensional Modeling

- Analyzed 99,441 orders across 7 source tables by building a dimensional warehouse with order-level payment and item aggregation to prevent double-counting.
- Identified 6,534 late deliveries among 96,470 eligible orders (6.77%); created seller scorecards and state-level reports to prioritize operational investigations.
- Built searchable BI reports, CSV exports, and Power BI import templates with DAX measures and documented KPI definitions.

Measured offline benchmark results, not production business impact. Review README and reproduce the experiment before presenting the work in an interview.
